The person behind the permanence

Art with precision. Work with meaning.

Renato Ostrowski is a Brazilian tattoo artist specializing in micro-realistic tattoos. With over seven years of experience, he has developed a distinctive language that combines realism, geometry and graphic interventions.

His work is recognized for clean, impressive results — especially after healing. Every new client and every story is an opportunity to push creative boundaries and create something original, personal and lasting.
